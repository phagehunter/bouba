# Changelog

Versions before 0.0.1 were development stages on one computer and were never published.

## 0.0.0-s8 (in progress)

- The web version is published to GitHub Pages on each push.

## 0.0.0-s7

- The app remembers recent names, the last language and a shortlist on the device.
- The address carries the name and language, and "Copy link" shares a result.
- The language is chosen from six market chips under the verdict. The drop-down is removed.
- Markets are grouped by band with no rank numbers. Tables become cards on phones.
- A grid of names by markets on the Compare screen.
- A printable summary, a skip link, larger touch targets, and line styles on marked letters in addition to color.
- The result updates a moment after typing stops.
- The app is named Bouba. It has a mark of two rounded forms, the Plum brand color, the bundled fonts Fredoka and Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, a loading visual and new app icons. The colored bands down the side of the verdict, the reason cards and the notes are removed.

## 0.0.0-s6

- Mandarin Chinese, Standard Arabic and Hindi, each with its own adapter, benchmark and tests (128 tests in all).
- The market table covers China, the Arab countries (19 countries added together) and India.
- Arabic script is shown right to left.
- The speaker-group switch is removed. The result is shown for the first group, and a note appears when the second group would say the name differently.

## Unreleased (0.0.0-s5)

- License files, third-party license texts, this changelog, the guidebook and issue forms.
- The web version can be installed to a phone's home screen and works offline.
- Desktop (Mac, Windows) and Android wrappers, built by GitHub Actions.

## 0.0.0-s4

- Burmese and Japanese added.
- Language selector, and a table ranking the covered countries for each name.
- American English spelling throughout.

## 0.0.0-s3

- The app screen: check a name, compare names, guide.
- Marked-up name, suggestions, a spoken version where the device has a voice.
- A copy edit to remove machine-written patterns, and a writing-style section in the contributor guide.

## 0.0.0-s2

- The engine, the Thai rules, the Thai benchmark and the first tests.

## 0.0.0-s1

- Research: similar tools, data sources, and the published work on Thai and Burmese.
